## Rules for picking free stock
- Use Pexels, Pixabay or Coverr. Check the licence on Videezy and Videvo clip by clip. Don't take footage from Pinterest.
- Avoid identifiable faces, since there are no model releases for ads. Also avoid visible logos and modern tech that breaks the 90s look.
- Always run it through `kit/tools/grade.sh` so it's edited and matches the style.
